# Launch Strategy — Viral Angles

## TikTok/Reels Demo Concepts

### 1. The Rescue Moment
*"I was about to scroll for an hour. This app gave me a 2-minute mission first."*
- Show: Phone about to open Instagram → INTENT notification → 2-minute mission → completed → intentional scroll
- Hook: "This app catches you before you waste time"

### 2. The Ugly First Move
*"I couldn't start my essay for 3 days. This app told me to write the worst sentence."*
- Show: Staring at blank doc → INTENT mission: "Write one ugly sentence" → writing → momentum
- Hook: "Perfectionism is procrastination in disguise"

### 3. The Salvage
*"I abandoned my mission after 30 seconds. This app didn't punish me."*
- Show: Abandoning mission → Salvage offer → 1-minute version → completion
- Hook: "This app doesn't punish failure. It salvages it."

### 4. The Drift Pattern
*"I found out I always avoid writing tasks after 3pm."*
- Show: Drift Graph insight → pattern revelation → experiment
- Hook: "Your phone knows your patterns better than you do"

### 5. The Comeback
*"I hadn't opened the app in 5 days. No guilt. Just a tiny restart."*
- Show: Comeback screen → no shame message → 2-minute mission → momentum restored
- Hook: "No guilt. Just a tiny restart."

## Target Audiences

### Students
- Angle: "Open assignment → one ugly sentence → rescued"
- Platforms: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
- Hashtags: #StudyTok #ADHD #Procrastination #ExamSeason

### Creatives
- Angle: "Ugly First Move for creative block"
- Platforms: Instagram, Twitter/X, YouTube
- Hashtags: #CreativeBlock #WritingCommunity #ArtistLife

### Professionals
- Angle: "Before you check Slack, do one tiny thing"
- Platforms: LinkedIn, Twitter/X, YouTube
- Hashtags: #Productivity #DeepWork #Focus

### ADHD/Neurodivergent
- **Careful**: Do NOT claim treatment. Say: "For people who struggle to start."
- Platforms: TikTok, Reddit, Twitter/X
- Hashtags: #ADHD #Neurodivergent #ExecutiveFunction

## Viral Features

### Shareable Rescue Card
"Almost drifted. Rescued 5 minutes."
- Shows: state, duration, result, anonymized category
- Privacy: No raw text by default

### Before Scroll Card
"Scrolled after a tiny win."
- Shows: 2-minute action done first

### Weekly Story Card
"This week: 7 rescued moments."
- Shows: rescued count, best protocol
- Privacy: No private task text

## Content Calendar (Pre-Launch)

### Week 1-2: Tease
- "Something that catches you before you waste time"
- Screenshots of the rescue screen
- Countdown to beta

### Week 3-4: Beta
- Invite-only beta
- Share rescue cards
- Collect testimonials

### Week 5-6: Launch
- TikTok/Reels blitz
- Product Hunt launch
- Reddit r/productivity, r/ADHD, r/getdisciplined
- Twitter/X thread: "How we built an anti-drift agent"

### Week 7-8: Sustain
- User stories
- Weekly story shares
- Drift pattern revelations (anonymized)

## Key Metrics

- Shares per user per week
- Share card conversion (views → installs)
- TikTok/Reels views → App Store page views
- Organic search volume for "anti procrastination app"
- App Store conversion rate
